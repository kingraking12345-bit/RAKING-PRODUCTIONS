Moderation tutorial

How to keep Community safe and fair.

1. Start with Reports

  1. Open Reports. Each card shows the reported content, who reported it, and how many other people reported the same thing.
  2. Remove deletes the content for everyone and tells the author. Warn sends a message without removing anything. Mute (hours) lets the member read but not post. Ban removes posting for the number of hours you enter, or permanently if left empty. Dismiss closes a report that does not break the rules.

2. Find content yourself

  1. Use Content to search recent posts and comments. You can restore anything removed by mistake, and pin up to 3 posts such as rules or news.

3. Members

  1. Look up a username to see their post history, reports against them, and past notices. Tick Also remove all their posts and comments when banning a spammer.
  2. Only the Creator can restrict other staff members.

4. Rules

  1. Slow mode limits how often each member can post. Read-only mode is an emergency brake. The announcement appears at the top of Community for everyone.
  2. Add blocked words to reject messages automatically.

5. Accountability

  1. Every action is recorded in the Activity log with your username. Members are notified when content is removed or they are restricted.
Moderation needs a Community employee account. Actions cannot be done by regular members.
