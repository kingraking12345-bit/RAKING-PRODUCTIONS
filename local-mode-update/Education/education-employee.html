      Education Employee Tutorial

      Create degrees, build courses, set prices and publish.

      1. Create degrees first

      1. Open Degrees and add the credentials your courses will award, for example “Grade 8 Mathematics Certificate” or “BSc Computing”.

      2. Build a course

      1. Open Courses. Choose the Level: Grade 1 to Grade 12, College or University.
      2. Add Lessons. Paste a YouTube link, write the notes, and attach documents or links. Tick Free preview on a lesson to let anyone watch it.
      3. Add Course materials (textbooks, cheat sheets, syllabus links).
      4. Add the Final exam questions and choose the correct answer for each. Set the pass mark.

      3. Set price and requirements

      1. Refane price: 0 = free. Any number above 0 is charged from the student's Refane balance when they unlock the course.
      2. Tick required degrees to lock a course until the student has earned them. You can combine a price and required degrees.
      3. Choose the degree awarded. Students earn it when they pass the final exam.

      4. Publish

      1. Open Publish, check the summary and publish. Use Preview my drafts first to see the real page.
      2. Paid and degree-gated course content (videos, notes, documents, exam questions) is stored privately on the server and only opens for students who unlocked the course. Exam answers are never sent to students.
      Drafts stay in this browser until you publish. Publishing needs an Education employee account.
