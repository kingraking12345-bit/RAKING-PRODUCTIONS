      Music Employee Tutorial

      Curate, build, tease and publish.

      Feature a track

      1. Open Featured tracks, enter title, artist, genre and a YouTube or SoundCloud link, then press Add track.
      2. Set the status to Draft to hide it until it is ready.

      Build a beat preset

      1. Open Beat presets, name the beat, set the tempo and click steps in the grid. Press Play to hear it.
      2. Press Add beat. Visitors can load it into the Studio sequencer.

      Set Refane prices

      1. Open Features & Refane pricing. 0 means free; any number above 0 costs that many Refane. Edit a price in the catalog and it is saved right away.
      2. Tracks and beat presets have their own Refane price field. Prices are informational until purchases are enabled.

      Tease a release and set a banner

      1. Use Upcoming releases for drops with a banner image and release date.
      2. Use Site banner for a one-line announcement at the top of the page.

      Publish

      1. Open Publish. Sign in on the main page with an account that has the Music Employee, Music Studio Director or Creator role.
      2. Check what changed, add a note and press Publish to everyone. You can restore any earlier version from the history.
      3. Use Preview my drafts to see your drafts on the real page before publishing.
      Drafts stay in this browser until you publish. Publishing needs a Music employee account.
