      Skills Employee Tutorial

      Create skills, require degrees, build courses and publish.

      1. Degrees come from Education

      1. Degrees are created and published by the Education team. They appear here automatically once published. If the list is empty, ask Education to publish their degrees first.

      2. Create a skill

      1. Open Skills, name the skill and tick the degrees required for it. A learner must hold all ticked degrees to open any course in that skill.

      3. Build courses

      1. Open Courses, pick the skill, and add lessons: a YouTube link, notes, and documents or links. Tick Free preview to let anyone watch a lesson.
      2. Optionally set a Refane price (0 = free) and extra degrees for just this course. There are no exams in Skills.

      4. Publish

      1. Open Publish, check the summary and publish. Locked course content is stored privately on the server and opens only after the learner passes the degree check and pays.
      Drafts stay in this browser until you publish. Publishing needs a Skills employee account.
